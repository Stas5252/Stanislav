# Experiments / hypotheses
Each experiment needs a clear baseline, change, measurement and no made-up uplift.
1. Hypothesis: a free working brief template attracts relevant information intent and brings visitors to the homepage brief. Measure guide impressions, organic clicks and assisted brief starts; status: implement / monitor.
2. Hypothesis: showing two real cases is better than three large cards. Change already live, but pre-change conversion baseline missing: cannot claim improvement.
3. Hypothesis: verified organic impressions will grow with better crawlable content. Measure after 30/60/90 days; do not attribute all change to one page without controls.
4. Do not A/B test intrusive form collection without consent and data protection review.
