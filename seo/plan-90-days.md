# SEO 90-day plan — 2026-10-28 to 2027-01-26
## Days 1–14 (technical + measurement)
- Baseline in audit-before.md and monthly-report.md.
- Update keyword mapping from real SERPs and connected GSC; do not infer volume from intent.
- Publish valuable, properly sourced how-to guide; verify 200/canonical/links on production.
- Inspect Search Console Sitemaps / Pages status; refresh /sitemap.xml through official UI if needed.
- Get Yandex Webmaster access; inspect diagnostic issues and RU national targeting.
- Audit page experience (LCP, INP, CLS); fix only reproducible bottlenecks.
## Days 15–30 (commercial proof)
- Map actual service queries and rank by intent and qualified lead likelihood.
- Upgrade existing pages with client proof, process detail and original screenshots; do not fake conversion lifts.
- Verify analytics consent, real contact event flow, Metrika reachGoal and CRM/lead counts.
- Prioritize one real industry landing based on client work and demonstrated query demand.
## Days 31–60 (growth)
- Editor-review and publish only approved content with unique value from content-calendar.csv.
- Offer two-way client references from public project sites where client approval exists.
- Seek genuine mentions in relevant publications and directories, no spam or bought links.
- Re-audit on mobile and compare search data in consistent time windows.
## Days 61–90 (optimization)
- Improve high-impression low-CTR pages only after enough real data exists.
- Remove duplicated keyword targeting, revise internal links, case proof and CTA based on attribution.
- Review Yandex/Bing/Google index coverage; evaluate any verified AI referrals.
- Monthly report: impressions, clicks, qualified leads, sales, indexed URLs, updates, issues.
The objective is qualified orders, not a guaranteed top ranking.
